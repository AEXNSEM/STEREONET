# Stereonet by Nsem

A free, mobile-friendly lower-hemisphere stereonet for structural geology, created by **Nsem**.

## Features
- Plot planes (strike/dip, dip direction/dip) and lines (trend/plunge); equal-area or equal-angle net
- 2D spin and 3D tumble, contouring, mean poles, per-set statistics
- Interpretation of your data at three levels: expert technical, standard technical, and simple
- Works offline once opened, and can be added to your phone's home screen

## Use it
Open the live link in this repository's About box, then use your browser's "Add to Home Screen".
