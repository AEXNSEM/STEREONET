Free Web Stereonet for structural geology

Created by Nsem

100% free · works in your browser · no sign-up · no download needed. Plot strike and dip, rotate the net in 2D or 3D, find fold axes, and get plain-language answers about folds, tilted beds and faults.

Android (Chrome): menu ⋮ → Install app. iPhone (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen.

Start here (3 steps)
  1. Enter your readings in the box further down: strike, dip and the direction it dips (e.g. 048, 45, SE). Add a distance if you have one. No data yet? Tap “Load sample”.
  2. Look at the net. Each curve is a layer and each dot is its pole. Tap any dot or curve to see its numbers and how to plot it by hand.
  3. Scroll to “Interpret my data” and tap Analyse. It answers the four lab questions three times (expert, standard, simple), with the evidence.
